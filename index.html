Quick-start tutorial

  1. Select ＋ LOAD on any deck and choose an audio file.
  2. Press PLAY; use the circular logo disc to scratch or move the playhead.
  3. Set the horizontal PITCH control, deck volume, EQ, and mixer fader.
  4. Open STEMS · VINYL · SLIP · CUES & LOOPS for performance pads.
  5. Open MORE TOOLS for effects, sampler, recording, MIDI, skins, and online-search links.

The stem pad splits frequency ranges; it does not perform AI vocal/instrument separation. Online-service searches open the providers' own sites and do not grant music licences.
